Hi! I'm a 4th year dual major in Physics and Computer Science at BITS Pilani. I'm most interested in AI for particle physics and problems where mathematical structure shows up. This led me to work with Prof. Dhruv Kumar on mechanistic interpretability of symmetry-aware models for the LHC and down a rabbit hole into mech interp more broadly.

I'm also part of the TAMBO Collaboration, working with Tommaso Dorigo and the MODE Collaboration on optimizing detector layouts using differentiable programming.

Currently I'm working at Wells Fargo as a Quant Intern in the Causal Inference team. I'm trying to learn a bit more about other mathematical concepts in finance too. Suprisingly, it's not very different!

In the past, I interned at DRDO's Quantum Technology division with Dr. Poornendu Chaturvedi to work on atomic frequency standards and then worked with Prof. Amol Holkundkar at BITS on simulating radiative recombination in intense laser fields.

I love studying Physics and Maths in general, Group Theory especially and have been trying to read more particle physics and QFT. I have a large backlog of almost-finished projects from the past year or two unfortunately :( This website is one of them. So, please don't mind if some things are not working :)
