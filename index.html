experience

I'm currently a visiting Researcher at the KEK Theory Center, Japan, working on AI for high-energy physics as part of my thesis at BITS Pilani under the supervision of Prof. Mihoko Nojiri.

I work with Prof. Dhruv Kumar in the PhantomGrad lab at BITS on mechanistic interpretability and distillation of symmetry-aware models for the LHC, which sent me down a rabbit hole into mech interp more broadly.

I'm also part of the TAMBO Collaboration, working with Tommaso Dorigo and the MODE Collaboration on optimizing detector layouts using differentiable programming for the TAMBO neutrino observatory.

Previously, I worked on atomic frequency standards and coherent population trapping at DRDO with Dr. Poornendu Chaturvedi, laser-assisted radiative recombination with Prof. Amol Holkundkar at BITS, and causal inference as a Quantitative Analyst at Wells Fargo.
